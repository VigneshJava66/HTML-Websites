1. Node Installation & License Covenants

By installing AgriNode v4 systems or solar sensor grids on your facility, you receive a non-exclusive, revocable license to execute our local micro-code. Any attempts to reverse-engineer local packet decoders or mock encryption hashes without our permission violates intellectual property covenants.

2. Automated Valve Safety Responsibility

The automated water trigger limits in your User Dashboard operate on cached micro-code. Farmers must periodically monitor physical valve mechanisms. AgriTech accepts no liability for water waste, crop dehydration, or flooding arising from sticking hardware valves, frozen pipes, or manual override mistakes.

3. Solar Panel & Battery Upkeep

Telemetry sensor battery lifetimes rely on clean solar panels. The farm coordinator is responsible for cleaning panel dust. Node battery drops resulting in latency warnings are logged inside the Admin Dashboard for troubleshooting support.

4. Telemetry Calibration Disclaimer

Soil moisture percentages, NPK recommendations, and micro-climate advisories are simulated trends based on localized sensor grids. Variations in soil type and temperature indices mean we cannot guarantee 100% accuracy for individual spot calibrations.
