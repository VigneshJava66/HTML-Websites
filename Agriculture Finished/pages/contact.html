Frequently Asked Questions

Important details about hardware warranties, data privacy, and calibration.

How long do the node sensor batteries last?

All AgriNode v4 telemetry systems include integrated miniature solar panels and a rechargeable LiFePO4 battery pack, yielding a baseline operating span of 5 to 7 years without manual recharging.

Who owns the yield data collected by the sensors?

You do. AgriTech strictly adheres to farm data privacy codes. Your localized node telemetry is encrypted end-to-end and stored securely. We do not sell or sharing individual farm profiles.

Do the water valves require a constant internet connection?

No. Valve controllers operate on local schedule models cached inside the firmware. If the internet connection drops, the micro-valves continue to water based on the last loaded soil threshold patterns.
