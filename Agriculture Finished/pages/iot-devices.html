Precision Hardware Ecosystem

We build robust IP67 weatherproof sensor systems certified for seasonal farm operations.

AgriNode v4 (Soil Probe)

Includes 3 subsoil sensors measuring humidity at depths of 10cm, 30cm, and 60cm. Communicates via LoRaWAN.

Accuracy Index: ±1.5% moisture
AgriHub Gateway (Mesh)

Receives pings from up to 250 local nodes within a 5km radius. Uplinks encrypted data via LTE or satellite.

Protocols: TLS 1.3 / AES-128
AgriValve LoRa Controller

Solar-assisted valve solenoid that opens and shuts water lines based on wireless dashboard calibrations.

Power Model: Solar harvesting

Resilient LoRa Mesh Networking

Our telemetry nodes do not rely on cellular towers inside crop zones. Instead, they form a self-healing wireless mesh. If Node 03 is blocked by a tractor, it automatically re-routes packets through Node 04 to reach the gate receiver.

Self-Healing Mesh Route: Active (AES Encrypted)

7-Year Battery Lifespans

By combining miniature monocrystalline solar arrays with deep-cycle rechargeable cells, AgriNode telemetry units run continuously. High energy storage ensures nodes transmit alerts even during consecutive weeks of heavy cloud cover or snow.

Precision Calibration Standards

Each node undergoes diagnostic loops before shipping to ensure precision calibration indices.

Thermal Equalization

Calibrated between -20°C and 60°C to prevent soil expansion metrics distortions.

Salinity Compensation

Adjusts dielectric parameters automatically to correct for high mineral salt levels.

Firmware Auto-Sync

Receives OTA updates via LoRa multicast to update moisture threshold metrics.
