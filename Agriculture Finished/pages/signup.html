1. Safe Password Standards

Our facility registry utilizes advanced checks. To create a secure account:

  • Must contain at least 8 characters
  • Include a mixture of numbers and special characters (e.g. #, !, $)
  • Avoid common crop names or generic calendar years

2. Cooperative Member Terms

AgriTech partners directly with local farming cooperatives. By registering your facility, you agree to coordinate environmental moisture metrics and participate in local drought safety procedures during alert conditions. Reviews of security policies are conducted annually.
