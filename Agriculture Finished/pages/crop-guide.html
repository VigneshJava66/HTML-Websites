Crops Moisture Requirements

Recommended optimal soil moisture bounds measured by AgriNode telemetry probes.

Wheat & Grains

Hardy species with moderate root depths. Prefers drier soil phases during grain ripening.

Optimal: 40% - 60%
Maize (Corn)

High transpiration rates. Requires deep moisture profiles during silk pollination phases.

Optimal: 55% - 75%
Soybeans

Requires stable nitrogen fixation levels and consistent moisture during pod development.

Optimal: 50% - 70%
Tomatoes

Greenhouse cultivar. Vulnerable to blossom-end rot if moisture levels spike or drop.

Optimal: 60% - 80%

Identify Mineral Deficiencies

Use this quick guide to diagnose soil test deficiencies before running calculations.

Nitrogen (N) Deficiency: Leaves turn pale green or yellow starting from the bottom foliage.
Phosphorus (P) Deficiency: Foliole margins develop purple shades; root development stymied.
Potassium (K) Deficiency: Leaf edges look scorched or brown; stalks grow thin and weak.

Organic Replenishment Recipes

Safe biological additives to resolve deficiencies without synthetic salt deposits.

Blood Meal (12-0-0)

Dehydrated animal blood. High nitrogen organic feed. Restores deep green foliage color. Apply 5 lbs per 100 sqft in wet soil phases.

Bone Meal (3-15-0)

Crushed skeletal base. Exceptional phosphorus source. Stimulates root growth and pollination blossom setups. Apply 4 lbs per 100 sqft.

Wood Ash (0-1-7)

Clean hardwood combustion residue. Provides potassium, calcium, and raises soil pH naturally. Apply 3 lbs per 100 sqft during fall cover.
