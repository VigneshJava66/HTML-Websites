Bio-Diverse Crop Rotations

Explore our organic crop rotation schedules designed to naturally break disease vectors and restore soil nutrients.

Cycle 1: Nitrogen Fixation

Clover, Alfalfa, and Legumes bind atmospheric nitrogen and release it into the subsoil through root nodules.

Months 1-4
Cycle 2: Biomass Accumulation

Sorghum, Mustard, and Rye accumulate dense root mass, increasing soil organic carbon when mulched.

Months 5-8
Cycle 3: Crop Yield Phase

High-demand grains (Spelt, Wheat) or vegetables take advantage of the rich, naturally fertilized soil matrix.

Months 9-12
Cycle 4: Regenerative Cover

Tillage radishes drill deep holes to aerate compacted soils while preparing ground for the next cycle.
